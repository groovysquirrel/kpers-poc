### **🎓 Lessons Learned**

The notes document key takeaways:
1. Enhanced background checks needed
2. Independent verification of performance critical  
3. More frequent on-site visits required
4. Faster action on warning signs essential
5. Even successful managers can turn bad (Stellar went from +28.7% to fraud)
6. Turnarounds ARE possible with right management response (Harbor Ridge, Northpoint)
7. Boring and reliable has value (Legacy Wealth)
